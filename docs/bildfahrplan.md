# Bildfahrplan (time–distance diagram)

`/bf` (index), `/bf/<corridor>?from=<hour>&h=<hours>`

## Data

Built by `vp run netz:build` from the **ÖBB GTFS** of the whole timetable year. Corridors are defined in `packages/netz/src/corridors.ts` by ordered anchor stations: **all ÖBB [Fahrplanbilder](https://www.oebb.at/de/fahrplan/fahrplanbilder)** that are trains in the ÖBB GTFS (61 of 83; private railways such as Mariazellerbahn, Pinzgaubahn, Zillertalbahn, StH/StB lines, cog and cable railways are not in the data), each under its ÖBB number (`/bf/101`), plus a few zugx corridors spanning several Fahrplanbilder (Westbahn, Südbahn, Koralmbahn, Giselabahn, Rheintal, S-Bahn trunk line). Old zugx corridor ids redirect to their Fahrplanbild (`/bf/brenner` → `/bf/300`); the build adds every station served between two anchors (station list taken from a reference day, Tuesday 2026-03-10), places stations by track distance (GTFS `shape_dist_traveled`, haversine fallback), and collects every train of the year that stops at ≥ 2 corridor stations, with its arrival/departure minutes per station. Runs with identical times on different days are stored once with a **running-days bitset** (same encoding as the train index), so any date of the timetable period can be shown. Details: [packages/netz/README.md](../packages/netz/README.md).

Limits: planned timetable (December snapshot; realtime via the live overlay below); dates outside the timetable period show no trains; ÖBB GTFS only (no WESTbahn/GKB/GySEV trains); trains passing a station without stopping get an estimated time there (passing points, below).

**Passing times**: stations a train runs through without stopping (its GTFS shape passes within 300 m) get an estimated time. The running time between its two stops is spread over the stretches in between by the time at the **permitted speed** (VzG data from OpenStreetMap, `packages/vzg`; stations linked by position, within 400 m), capped at the category's top speed (RJX/RJ 230, ICE 250, EC/IC/WB 200, D/NJ/EN/CJX/REX 160, R/S 140 km/h; assumptions). So a train is later in slow sections (Wienerwald, 65–100 km/h) and earlier on fast ones (Tullnerfeld, up to 160). Stretches without VzG data get the average speed of the known ones; no data at all: by distance. No acceleration/braking yet. Speed limits © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

## How it is shown

- **Index** (`/bf`): all corridors (sorted by ÖBB Fahrplanbild number, searchable by number) with main stations, length, stations, trains/day (on the reference Tuesday) and the mix of train categories; filter by corridor or station name, sortable.
- **Diagram**: x = km along the corridor (major stations bold), y = time downwards. Each train is a polyline through its stops (vertical segments = dwell time), coloured by category; steeper = slower. Regional/S-Bahn lines are thinner.
- **Section and direction**: pick the first and last station (`?a=`, `?b=` station ids) to show only part of the route; ⇄ swaps the direction (`?rev=1`, the diagram is mirrored); "Whole route" resets.
- **Date** (`?d=YYYY-MM-DD`, default today): date picker, ‹ › for previous/next day; only trains running on that date are drawn, with the train count.
- Window: scroll (mouse wheel / trackpad) over the diagram to move in time (10 % of the window per notch), Ctrl/⌘ + scroll or pinch to zoom (1–24 h), Shift + scroll stays horizontal; or use start hour ▲▼ and 2/4/8/24 h; the dashed red line is the current time (only when today is shown). Category chips hide/show categories.
- Hover highlights a train (number and destination below the chart); click opens its live train run on the selected date.
- **Wikidata**: the railway lines of a sheet (after "km · stations") are resolved at build time from Wikidata — each station by its IBNR (P954 = our HAFAS id), its connecting lines (P81), with the ÖBB line number (P1671) where present — and link to [DinoData](https://dinodata.toolforge.org). Clicking a station name opens a small menu: departure board, or the station on Wikidata (DinoData `#P954=<id>`). The station board links to Wikidata the same way. No Wikidata ids are hard-coded.
- **Live overlay** ("Aramis für Arme"; button "Echtzeit"/"Live", `?live=1`, off by default, only for today): realtime paths from ÖBB HAFAS (Scotty) on top of the planned ones (`apps/web/src/lib/bf-live.ts`). No per-train requests:
  - _Past_: one departure board (StationBoard) per major station of the shown section, plus arrival boards at the section's two ends, once per window (≈ 12 requests on the whole Westbahn); each board lists every train there with its actual time. Boards with ≥ 200 trains are paged.
  - _Now_: one radar request (JourneyGeoPos) over the section per minute (paused while the tab is hidden): realtime of the stops around every running train, also at minor stations.
  - _Path_: the GTFS path (incl. passing points) with realtime where known and planned + the last known delay in between (never earlier than the event before). Matching by category + train number (`RJX 162`; HAFAS `s` → `S`). Trains not in the GTFS — WESTbahn, ÖBB trains renumbered since the GTFS snapshot — are drawn through the stations they were seen at, if those span ≥ 3 corridor stations or ≥ 10 km (not e.g. the Südbahn S-Bahn between Wien Hbf and Meidling); their planned path is dotted.
  - _Display_: the planned path of a train with live data becomes thin and faint (trains without live data keep their normal planned line): actual solid up to now, forecast dashed, a dot at every time reported by Scotty (between dots: planned + delay); trains not in the timetable data have a dotted planned path. Current delay at "now" (amber < 5′, red ≥ 5′). A legend below the chart explains the styles; hover adds the delay and where the last report came from ("Echtzeit an 3 Bahnhöfen, zuletzt St. Pölten Hbf 16:55").
  - _Limits_: between major stations the past is interpolated (planned + delay), exact minor-station times only while the train was on the radar during the session; trains showing only a line number in HAFAS ("CJX 5") are not matched.
