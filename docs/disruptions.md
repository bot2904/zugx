# Disruptions

`/him`

## Data

HAFAS `HimSearch` (live): the current HIM messages (HAFAS Information Manager) — construction work, rail replacement, delays, incidents.

## How it is shown

Newest first (by last modification), with title, affected section (from – to station), validity period and full text. The filter searches title, text and station names. Messages attached to individual trains also appear on boards, connections and train runs (⚠).
